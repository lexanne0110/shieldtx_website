Radius & spacing

Three radii: 16, 12 and 8. The radius steps down by 4 for each level of nesting, so inner corners stay parallel to outer ones.

Motion

Motion

Opening loader. The solid shield arrives, contracts into the four-band mark, then the bands flip open on alternating sides. 2.8s.

Where it runs

Website on page open, and the opening of ShieldTX decks. Skipped for reduced motion.

Easing

--ease-mech cubic-bezier(.65,.02,.18,1) for short mechanical moves. --ease-open cubic-bezier(.36,.02,.2,1) for reveals.

Restraint

Motion explains, never decorates. Every animation respects reduced motion and stops when off screen.
