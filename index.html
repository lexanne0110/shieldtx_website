The Problem

Your wallet exposes everything.

  • 01

    Everything is public

    Your full P&L, position history, and account size are visible to everyone the moment you place a trade.

  • 02

    Profits leak to copy traders

    Tools like HyperDash and Dexly mirror your trades within seconds of execution and copy traders erode your profitability.

  • 03

    Enterprises are left out

    Trading desks and vaults with substantial size cannot use a venue where competitors read your book, every hour of every day.

What you do today

Today's workarounds
fail.

  • 01

    Multiple wallets

    Fractures your workflow. Managing gas, seed phrases, and transaction history across wallets adds operational overhead with no upside.

  • 02

    Wallet clustering still wins

    Arkham and Nansen cluster your different wallets through temporal correlation. Same asset, same direction, seconds apart is enough to link them.

  • 03

    CEX routing

    Hands over custody of your assets and leaves the venue. You lose self-custody and on-chain composability for shielding that should be native.
