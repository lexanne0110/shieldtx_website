Methodology

Cost figures are estimated from observed copy-bot fill behaviour against top-100 HL wallets by PnL over 2–9 November 2026. “bps per trade” = median adverse drift, in basis points, between copied and matched uncopied bursts, controlling for coin, size and volatility. Dollar estimates translate those bps against the notional traded in copied bursts. Full methodology available here. Figures are estimates, not guarantees, and are updated each issue.
