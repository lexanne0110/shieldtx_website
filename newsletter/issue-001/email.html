Every week we count what copying costs the traders at the top of Hyperliquid. This period the bill was $14.2M, and most of the people paying it never saw the bots coming.

 
02 · The trend

Is copying costing traders more?

Copying is not new, but it is getting faster and cheaper to do. Every period the bots get a little quicker to the fill and a little better at spotting the trades worth following.

Volume has eased 3% over the same stretch. The traders on the other side are moving a little less and paying a little more for every trade they make.

06 · Speed and size

The mechanism: why copying hurts.

The mechanism is simple. The average copy-bot fills 340 milliseconds after the leader, fast enough to trade on the same move the leader just created.

Size is the second part of the problem. Only 12% of positions under $50K were copied, against 64% of positions above $250K. The bigger the trade, the more it is worth following, and the more it costs to be followed.

Methodology

Cost figures are estimated from observed copy-bot fill behaviour against top-100 HL wallets by PnL over 2–9 November 2026. “bps per trade” is the median adverse drift, in basis points, between copied and matched uncopied bursts, controlling for coin, size and volatility. Dollar estimates translate those bps against the notional traded in copied bursts. Figures are estimates, not guarantees, and are updated each issue.
