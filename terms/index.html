About Shield TX

Shield TX is a blockchain-based user-interface and middleware solution enabling users to interact with third-party trading protocols, including Hyperliquid, through privacy-enhancing transaction structures. The Platform facilitates the routing of user-initiated transactions through dynamically generated wallets and internal accounting mechanisms (including "Shielded Notes") designed to reduce on-chain traceability of trading activity. Shield TX is intended to operate as a technical facilitator and to minimise custodial control over user assets to the extent supported by the Platform's architecture. Certain operational components, including server-managed wallets and off-chain accounting, are necessary to support the Platform's privacy and execution design.
